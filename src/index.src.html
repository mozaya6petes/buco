BUCO Burgers & Cocktails

Comfort food, taken seriously.

Reserve a table View the menu

Burgers. Cocktails. That’s the point.

BUCO is a modern gastro bar in the heart of Beirut, built around one simple idea: take the food everybody knows and make it worth remembering.

Premium ingredients, serious technique and cocktails made to stand beside the food — without turning dinner into something formal.

The chef

From Noma to Beirut.

Chef Tarek Alameddine opened BUCO in late 2023, bringing the precision of fine dining into food that doesn’t need an introduction.

Before coming home, he spent years as a sous chef at Noma in Copenhagen.
