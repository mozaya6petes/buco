We want everyone to be able to use this website and visit BUCO.

The website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site:

  • can be used fully with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link;
  • uses text colours that meet AA contrast levels against their backgrounds;
  • describes images with alternative text;
  • labels every form field and explains errors in words, not only colour;
  • turns off motion effects if your device is set to reduce motion;
  • works when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.

Known limitations

External services we link to (such as Google Maps, Instagram or a booking system) are run by other companies and may not meet the same standard.
